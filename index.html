How to get coordinates from Google Maps:
1. Open Google Maps on your phone
2. Long-press any spot on the map (property corner)
3. Tap the coordinates that appear at the bottom
4. Tap Share → copy the link, OR just note the lat/lon shown
5. Paste the link OR the coordinates below
